Haymaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Haymaker is a game you play on your own device. This page explains what the game does and does not do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Haymaker has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your coins, unlocked fighters, gloves, player names and fight records are saved only in your device's storage so you can keep your progress. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Fonts

The first time the game opens with an internet connection, it downloads its lettering fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this lets Google's servers see your device's IP address. See Google's privacy policy. After that, the fonts are saved on your device.

Vibration

On supported phones the game uses vibration during hits. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Children

Haymaker does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
